Pieces

    Walls are drawn to scale from sample measurements. Tap a piece to see where its nail goes.

    275 pieces from Unsplash, credited to each photographer and shown under the Unsplash License. They are samples, not for sale here. Each one is measured for color, brightness, contrast, busyness and empty space, and that's what the taste test learns from.

    Layouts come from the Walldrobe engine: hanging rules for size, height and spacing, then every wall is judged whole: how well it fits, your taste, its colors (the scheme on the color wheel, how much of each color, whether accents repeat) and its design (balance, a clear focal piece, busy next to quiet, side pieces facing in). Keep the pieces you like and refresh the rest.
