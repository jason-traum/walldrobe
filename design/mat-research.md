# What comes with a mat: research for Walldrobe (US, checked 2026-10-04)

Why: Walldrobe decides which pieces get a mat and tells you what to buy. This checks what a normal person actually gets when a frame says "with mat", what a mat hides, and what to do when the mat that comes in the box is for the wrong print. Sources are the brands' own pages and product data (Americanflat, Golden State Art, IKEA, Hobby Lobby, Jerry's Artarama, Frame It Easy, Level Frames). Amazon and Michaels block reading; anything not confirmed is marked unverified.

## The short answer

- A frame sold "with mat" has the mat already in it. It lifts out, so the same frame holds a full-size print without it. Americanflat: "Most matted frames can be used without the mat." IKEA lists both sizes on each page. Walmart titles read "Display 8x10 with Mat or 11x14 without Mat".
- The mat's print is about one standard size down from the frame (Americanflat: about 4 in smaller each way, a 2 in border).
- The window is a half inch smaller than the print it's "for": an 8 x 10 mat's window is 7.5 x 9.5. A quarter inch of the print hides on each side, so a signature or text at the very edge gets covered. Americanflat says order the print at the mat's size, don't size down. IKEA sometimes hides more (a LOMVIKEN 12 x 16 shows 7 x 9 of an 8 x 10).
- Ready-made mats are white (some off-white), 4-ply, acid-free on the better lines. Black and double mats exist but are rare in budget frames.
- Turn the frame and the mat turns with it: mats work portrait or landscape.
- A mat can be bought on its own for a frame that came without one: the mat's outside equals the frame size. About $3 at Hobby Lobby (11 x 14 for 8 x 10, plain cardboard), about $10 acid-free with a backing board (Jerry's Artarama, Crescent), about $1.50 each in 25-packs (Golden State Art).
- Big frames mostly come without a mat. Americanflat's 24 x 36 is an unmatted poster frame; a matted 24 x 36 print needs a 28 x 40 frame. IKEA's RODALM 24 x 36 is the exception (mat for 50 x 70 cm). No source states a cutoff; the pattern is around 24 x 36.
- Tape the print to the back of the mat at the top edge only (acid-free tape, a T-hinge for big ones). Taping all four sides makes it buckle.

## Gaps filled (second pass, Oct 4)

- Square frames hold smaller prints than the table guessed: Americanflat's 16x16 is matted for 5x7 and its 20x20 for 8x10 or 10x10 (wide mats). For a 12x12 print: Upsimples 16x16, Americanflat Hudson 16x16 ($20.99) or Streamline 15x15.
- Michaels precut mats (Studio Decor): 8x10 for 5x7 $4.99, 11x14 for 8x10 $6.49, 16x20 for 11x14 $9.79, 18x24 for 12x18 $16.49; double mats cost more.
- Wide (oversized) mats come ready-made at Americanflat (16x20 for 6x8, 20x20 for 8x10, 24x24 for 11x14...), mostly in sets ($108 for a pair of 16x20s). Cheaper: a plain frame and a custom precut mat.
- Frame colors: white usually costs the same as black; light wood and gold are often a different, dearer line (IKEA RODALM wood +$5; Target's gold is its Thin Metal line, $40 at 16x20). Prices per color are in web/framers.js.
- Wide moulding (about 1.5 in) is cheap only at IKEA (EDSBRUK: 8x10 $16.99, 12x16 $22.99, 16x20 $29.99, 20x28 $39.99); Framebridge's wide styles start at $110.

## What Walldrobe does with this

- Each size's mat pairing (web/main.js PRINT_IN) uses the most common one; a seller whose mat is for another print shows "mat 12 x 16" in the table, and the cheap fix is a precut mat.
- Americanflat's 16x20 now pairs with 12x16 (was 11x14).
- The drawing shows the mat's window, a half inch smaller than the print.
- Where to frame says the mat comes in the frame, what it hides, and what a precut mat costs. Hang it adds the top-edge tape step for matted frames.
- The engine keeps big frames (30 in or more on the long side) and the statement piece plain unless you pick All.
